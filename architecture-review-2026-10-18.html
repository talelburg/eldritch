Problem. Every frame's payload knows who must answer it (13 kinds name one investigator, 3 name the lead, 2 have several answerers), and every request drops it. In a two-seat game the mulligan, commit and hand-size prompts don't say whose hand, two reaction options on one location are indistinguishable (#1024), and the server cannot check a seat (#581). Six kinds build their prompt only when pushed, so they cannot be re-surfaced, the harness cannot settle on them, and a native exit that reaches one suspends after writing.

Solution. One exhaustive per-frame answer to "who answers and what is asked"; push sites and drive arms stop assembling requests, and apply_via builds the outcome from the top frame. Requests name their responder; an option offered on someone's behalf names them; a PickMultiple lists its eligible candidates as anchored options with its count. The lead gets one named function, separate from "first in player order". Prompt text uses names, not InvestigatorId(1). Slice 1 (any time): rebuild the six push-time prompts from their frames, so no exit ever returns AwaitingInput. Slice 2: the responder, behind an ADR.
