# The Necronomicon (01009)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-04-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01009>

- If you add this to your hand without drawing (e.g. via [Research Librarian](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01032)), the **Revelation** ability still triggers.

- Moving damage or horror is different from 'dealing' or 'taking' damage or horror, so does not trigger effects or allow the player controlling Daisy Walker to reassign the horror.

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata for misattributed illustrators, so the ArkhamDB illustrator attribution and the card image above differ. The ArkhamDB attribution is the correct one - April 2025.
