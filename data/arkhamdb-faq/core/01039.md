# Deduction (01039)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-03-10; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01039>

- The word 'Additional' means 'in addition to other clues you discover', i.e. it modifies the number of clues that you would find, it does not add an extra effect on top of any other effects (e.g. does not stack with [Burglary](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01045)).

- If you commit Deduction to another player's investigation attempt, that other player would discover 1 additional clue, not you.
