# Arcane Initiate (01063)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-03-09; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01063>

- When you use the ability to search the top 3 cards, if there is a ***Spell*** among them, you must draw it, even if it is [Dark Memory](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01013). You cannot choose to fail to find what you're searching for if it's there.
