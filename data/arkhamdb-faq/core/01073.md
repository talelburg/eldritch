# Scavenging (01073)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-01-30; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01073>

- You can successfully **Investigate** a location even if there are no clues on it.

- Success is determined in Step 6 of the [Skill Test Timing](../../rules-reference/rules/Appendix_II_Timing_and_Gameplay.md#skill-test-timing), and committed cards get discarded in Step 8, so you cannot use Scavenging to get back an ***Item*** card that you've just committed to the same investigation attempt.
