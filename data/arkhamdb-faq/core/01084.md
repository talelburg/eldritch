# Lucky! (01084)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2016-12-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01084>

- The "When you would fail a skill test" triggering condition comes before the "After you fail a skill test" triggering condition, so you cannot play Lucky! after you use [Rabbit’s Foot](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01075) or play ["Look what I found!"](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01079).

- If you reveal an Auto-Fail token, your total skill value is considered to be 0, all modifiers are ignored.
