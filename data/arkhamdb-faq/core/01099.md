# Psychosis (01099)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-05-03; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01099>

- Any investigator at the same location as the investigator with [Psychosis](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01099) in their threat area may trigger the [action][action] to discard [Psychosis](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01099), as per the FAQ [V1.0, section 2.1].

- Triggers even if you assign horror to an asset you control (you're still taking horror, even if you reassign it).
