# Hypochondria (01100)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-05-03; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01100>

- Any investigator at the same location as the investigator with [Hypochondria](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01100) in their threat area may trigger the [action][action] to discard [Hypochondria](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01100), as per the FAQ [V1.0, section 2.1].

- Triggers even if you assign damage to an asset you control (you're still taking damage, even if you reassign it).
