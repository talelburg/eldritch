# Graveyard (01133)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-01-06; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01133>

- If there are any ready enemies in the Graveyard when you move there, they will engage you even before the **Forced** ability resolves. So if you fail the test and choose to move to Rivertown, they will move with you.

- If a card effect instructs you to move, you do not need to spend an action, and it doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity.
