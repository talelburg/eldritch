# "Wolf-Man" Drew (01137)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01137>

- If you play [Dodge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01023) or [Hypnotic Gaze](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02153) on ["Wolf-Man" Drew](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01137)'s attack, it interrupts the initiation of the **Forced** effect and neither attack nor forced effect triggers.
