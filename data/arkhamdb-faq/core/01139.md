# Peter Warren (01139)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2016-11-27; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01139>

- Using the **Parley** ability to add this enemy to your victory display does not count as defeating this enemy (for purposes of [Roland](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01001)'s ability, [Evidence!](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01022), etc).

- Defeating this enemy as normal will also add it to your victory display.
