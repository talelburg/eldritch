# Ruth Turner (01141)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-03-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01141>

- Adding this enemy to the victory display via its **Forced** ability does not count as defeating this enemy (for purposes of [Roland](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01001)'s ability, [Evidence!](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01022), etc).

- Defeating this enemy as normal will also add it to your victory display.
