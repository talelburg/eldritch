# Disrupting the Ritual (01148)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-01-06; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01148>

- First you spend 1 clue by returning it to the token bank. Then, if you succeed the test, place 1 clue on the act card from the token bank.

- Your investigator doesn't need to be at the [Ritual Site](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01156) in order to activate the ability of this act card.
