# Crypt Chill (01167)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-02-23; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01167>

- You must discard as asset from play. Even though you control cards in your hand, deck, and discard pile, there's also this rule:
[Ability](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Ability.md)

*"Card abilities only interact with other cards that are in play, unless the ability specifically references an interaction with cards in an out-of-play area."*

- Note that when assets run out of charges, they don't automatically get discarded unless they specifically say so.

- You cannot choose and discard [The Necronomicon](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01009) or [Baron Samedi](https://arkhamdb.com/card/99003) because they 'cannot leave play'.
