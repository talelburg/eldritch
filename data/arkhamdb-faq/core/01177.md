# Yithian Observer (01177)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-05-09; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01177>

- If you play a card while engaged with Yithian Observer, it will make an attack of opportunity against you and resolve its **Forced** ability, after you pay the cost of the card but before you resolve its effects or put it into play. If the ability makes you discard that card at random, you’ll end up having wasted resources without gaining anything in return.

- If you play [Dodge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01023) or [Hypnotic Gaze](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02153) on [Yithian Observer](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01177)'s attack, it interrupts the initiation of the **Forced** effect and neither attack nor forced effect triggers.
