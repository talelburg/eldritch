Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-07-27; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/01684>

- ~~**Q:** For cards like [Deny Existence](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05032), ["I've had worse…"](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05315), [Delay the Inevitable](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05021), etc, do they directly or indirectly work against effects that do not deal damage and horror directly to your investigator (e.g. [Snake Bite](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04080)) or deal direct damage to other things as well as your investigator (e.g. [Centuries of Secrets](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05099), [Ceremony Room](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05214))? **A:** Delay the Inevitable, I've Had Worse and Deny Existence only work when *you* (aka your investigator) are being dealt damage/horror. If damage or horror is being dealt directly to a card other than your investigator, you cannot use Deny Existence, Delay the Inevitable, or I've Had Worse to cancel/ignore it.~~ This ruling has been overturned and, at the time of updating (27/07/22), we await an FAQ update to bring this ruling in line with FAQ entry 2.12, [Interpreting "You" When Taking or Being Dealt Damage](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/You_Your.md#interpreting-you-when-taking-or-being-dealt-damage-added-in-faq-section-card-ability-interpretation-point-212-updated-faq-v25). The information we currently have is: 
 - According to our current rulings, “you” are “dealt damage” when your investigator or an asset you control is dealt damage.
 - This does mean that, if you activated the ability on [Beat Cop](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01028) while you had [Hypochondria](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01100) in your threat area, “you” would have been dealt damage, and you must then take 1 direct horror.
 - This also means that the previous ruling on Delay the Inevitable and similar cards has been overturned; those cards would count damage dealt to your assets as damage dealt “to you.”
