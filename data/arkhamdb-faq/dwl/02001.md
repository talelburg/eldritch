# Zoey Samaras (02001)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-01-02; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02001>

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata from the Arkham Horror FAQ in its card text, so the ArkhamDB text and the card image above differ, as the ArkhamDB text has been edited to contain this erratum (updated January 2022): **Erratum:** [Zoey Samaras](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02001)'s **Deckbuilding Options** should read: "... up to five other level 0 cards from any other classes ([seeker], [rogue], [mystic], and/or [survivor])." - FAQ, v.1.5, April 2019

- If [Zoey Samaras](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02001) is dealing with a Massive enemy, the enemy is “considered” engaged with you, so it will enable abilities – like the [Machete](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01020)’s – that require you to be engaged with an enemy. However, there is no timing point of engagement, you’re just “considered” engaged with it, so you can’t really trigger reactions to becoming engaged with the enemy, like [Zoey Samaras](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02001) or [Zoey's Cross](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02006).

- **Q:** If a swarming enemy engages Zoey Samaras, can she trigger her  [reaction] ability once for each enemy in total, or only once for the entire swarm? **A:** Although swarming enemies move and engage together as a single entity, each swarm card is its own separate enemy. If a swarming enemy engages Zoey, each of its swarm cards are also enemies that have engaged Zoey. Therefore, she may trigger her [reaction] ability once for each of them. - FAQ, v.1.9, June 2021
