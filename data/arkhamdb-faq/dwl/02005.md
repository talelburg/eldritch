# "Ashcan" Pete (02005)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-02-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02005>

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata from the Arkham Horror FAQ in its card text, so the ArkhamDB text and the card image above differ, as the ArkhamDB text has been edited to contain this erratum (updated January 2022): **Erratum:**  [Ashcan Pete](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02001)'s **Deckbuilding Options** should read: "... up to five other level 0 cards from any other classes ([guardian], [seeker], [rogue], and/or [mystic])." - FAQ, v.1.5, April 2019

- You don't need to pay [Duke](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02014)'s cost when you start the game with him in play.

- **Q:** I'm not sure how to read the rules for parallel Pete compared to the rules for attachments leaving play. As an example, if an enemy has [Handcuffs](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04265) attached, and the enemy is defeated, both the enemy and Handcuffs are discarded. Are the Handcuffs discarded while attached to a scenario card, or does the enemy discard; and the handcuffs then discard while not attached to anything? **A:** In the situation described, you would return Handcuffs to your hand with Parallel Ashcan Pete. It was considered attached to a scenario card upon being discarded. (Rules Form, December 2023)

- **Q:** I'm playing parallel ["Ashcan" Pete](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02005) and my [Makeshift Trap](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09100) in play has the Explosive Device upgrade with no “time” left. Can I use Pete’s ability to return it to my hand and still deal damage? **A:** No. Using Parallel Pete’s [reaction] ability on Makeshift Trap replaces the discarding of Makeshift Trap, meaning its Explosive Device upgrade ability cannot resolve. (FAQ v2.2, February 2024)
