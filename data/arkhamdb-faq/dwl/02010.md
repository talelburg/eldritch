# Jenny's Twin .45s (02010)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-09-26; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02010>

- If you use [Sleight of Hand](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03029) to put [Jenny's Twin .45s](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02010) into play, you never pay the cost so you never specify what X is. (“If X is not defined, its value is equal to 0.” [RR, page 22](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/The_letter_X.md)) Same goes for while Jenny’s Twin .45s are in your hand. After it is played, its cost is no longer defined, so it reverts back to being undefined (“X”, or “0”). Essentially, the cost of Jenny’s Twin .45s is only defined while you are playing it.
