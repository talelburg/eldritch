# Double or Nothing (02026)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-04-18; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02026>

- First determine the results of the successful test (including any additional effects provided by cards, e.g. bonus damage, effects of committed skill cards, etc.), then resolve these effects twice.

- For [Blinding Light](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01069) and similar cards, the *"if a special symbol is revealed..."* part will not be doubled, because it is not the effect of the successful test.

- Do not double any **Reactions** to a successful skill test (e.g. [Dr. Milan Christopher](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01033), [Scavenging](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01073)).

- All cards committed to a test are committed simultaneously. This means you cannot use [Double or Nothing](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02026) to double the difficulty of a test and then commit [Rise to the Occasion](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02192) based off the doubled difficulty of the test.

- **Q:** Does [Double or Nothing](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02026) work with extra action effects like [Quick Thinking](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02229) or the level 2 [.41 Derringer](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03234)? **A:** Double or Nothing works with both of those effects! Those effects indeed trigger during step 7 [of a skill test], and then create a delayed effect which grants the extra action(s) after the test resolves.
