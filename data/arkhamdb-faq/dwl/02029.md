# Ritual Candles (02029)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2021-07-14; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02029>

- **Q:** I have [Ritual Candles](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02029) in play and take a test. Step 3 occurs, where I pull a [cultist] symbol. I trigger Candles as a [reaction]. Step 4 occurs, and the [cultist] effect is to reveal another token. I return to step 3, where I pull a [skull]. Can I trigger Ritual Candles again? (I know you can't when you reveal multiple tokens simultaneously (say through [Olive McBride](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04197)) but I'm less certain here.) **A:** You sure can! Actually, you can even when revealing multiple tokens with Olive as well, as long as they aren’t being ignored/canceled. For example, if with Olive you revealed a [skull], a [tablet] and an [elder_thing], and then ignored the [elder_thing], you could still trigger Ritual Candles twice—once for the [skull], and once for the [tablet]. 

- **Q:** [*part 2, following on from previous Q&A*] I thought you can't with Olive because of this rule on [reaction] triggered abilities: "Each [reaction] triggered ability may be triggered only once each time the specified condition on the ability is met. For example, an ability that is triggered "After X occurs," may be used once each time "X" occurs. Additionally the FAQ entry [Resolving Multiple Revealed Chaos Tokens](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Chaos_Tokens.md#resolving-multiple-revealed-chaos-tokens-added-in-faq-section-card-ability-interpretation-point-25): "[...] when resolving multiple chaos tokens, any game or card effects which trigger if a certain chaos token is revealed—such as the text “If the named chaos token is revealed during this skill test…” on [Recall the Future](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04158)—will trigger if any of the resolved chaos tokens meet the specified conditions. Such an effect will not trigger twice if two of the designated tokens are resolved." **A:** [*part 2*] Each [reaction] ability can be triggered once each time the specified condition is met. In this case the condition is “after a (symbol) token is revealed during a skill test.” Therefore, if two symbols are revealed during a skill test, the reaction may be triggered twice. (This would be different if it read “1 or more tokens.”) As for the second ruling [the FAQ], that doesn’t apply here. That ruling is specifically talking about effects within another ability that trigger if a chaos token is revealed, such as the horror-dealing effect on [Shrivelling](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01060). Those are not independent reaction triggers, they are delayed effects that trigger if a particular type of chaos token is revealed (in other words, they are a binary state; they either trigger or they don’t).
