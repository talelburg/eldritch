# Light of Aforgomon (02085)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-04-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02085>

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata for misattributed illustrators, so the ArkhamDB illustrator attribution and the card image above differ. The ArkhamDB attribution is the correct one - April 2025.

- **Q:** Does [Light of Aforgomon](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02085) leave play immediately when the Act or Agenda it is attached to flips to its b side, or only when the a side of the next act/agenda is reached? **A:** Light of Aforgomon will leave play when the attached act/agenda leaves play (which is typically after resolving its ‘b’ side).
