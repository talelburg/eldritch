Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-03-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02088>

- **Q:** I have a question about [Unhallowed Country](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02088). Does its second line of text remove traits from allies as well, including the ***Ally*** trait itself? I compared the language on this card to [Mind Wipe](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01068), [Stubborn Detective](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01103), [Threads of Reality](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06100) which all have "except for Traits" language. **A:** Thank you for bringing this to our attention. Unhallowed Country should have been given a wording such as Mind Wipe, where the text box is treated as blank except for Traits. Please apply this same logic to Unhallowed Country; your Ally cards will remain Ally cards for the duration of the ability.
