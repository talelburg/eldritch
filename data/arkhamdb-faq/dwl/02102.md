# Arcane Barrier (02102)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02102>

- When taking the [willpower] test moving *into* [Arcane Barrier](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02102)'s location, you are considered to be taking the test from your *starting* location. Investigators at your starting location may commit cards to help you pass the test, but investigators at the location with the [Arcane Barrier](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02102) may not. 

- If your location has an [Arcane Barrier](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02102) and a connected location has an [Arcane Barrier](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02102), you must test [willpower] twice if moving between those two locations. If you fail the first test and opt to 'cancel the effects of the move', you do not take the second [willpower] test. "It is as though the move never occurred, so any hanging Forced abilities or reactions that would trigger based on you moving (such as the second Arcane Barrier) do not trigger." [Matt Newman]
