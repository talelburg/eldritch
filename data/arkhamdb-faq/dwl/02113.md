# Oops! (02113)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02113>

- [Oops!](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02113) does not specify to deal the damage an attack would have dealt if it had been successful, so the damage dealt should be the damage the attack would deal if it fails (for instance, an Oops!'d [.41 Derringer](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01047) attack deals 1 damage). 

- [Oops!](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02113) vs [Shotgun](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01029): if you play Oops! on a failed attack with Shotgun, the damage dealt is 1.
