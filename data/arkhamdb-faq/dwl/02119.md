# Restricted Access (02119)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-05-02; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02119>

- While resolving the B side of this agenda (A Creature of the Void), the A side (Restricted Access), as well as the A side of Agenda 2, are not active. If this is the first time you spawn the [Hunting Horror](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02141), it will not have [Shadow-spawned](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02142) attached to it. If [Shadow-spawned](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02142) is already attached, it will not gain a resource at this juncture.
