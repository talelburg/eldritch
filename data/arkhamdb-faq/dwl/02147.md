# Bandolier (02147)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02147>

- If you lose an asset that grants you additional slots, you must discard cards in excess of those slots until you have a legal number of assets in play in those slots. For example, if you had a [Bandolier](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02147), a [Shotgun](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01029) and a [Machete](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01020) in play, and the bandolier was discarded, you would be forced to discard either the shotgun or the machete.

- Slots are a constant restriction on the cards you can have in play, and something the game state constantly checks.
