# Dining Car (02173)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02173>

- Setup for The Essex County Express tells you to ignore 'upon entry' effects. The Dining Car's effect is not 'upon entry', so if this is the first location, the investigators must find and draw a [Grappling Horror](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02182). The lead investigator may choose who draws this enemy.
