# Emergency Cache (02194)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-01-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02194>

- **Q:** What happens if I play [Emergency Cache](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02194) and the drawn card is [Paranoia](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01097)? **A:** "Level 2 Emergency Cache reads 'Gain 3 resources and draw 1 card.' This effect is resolved simultaneously, so you would gain 3 resources and draw Paranoia simultaneously, then resolve Paranoia and lose the just-gained resources. Sorry!"
