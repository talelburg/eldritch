# Blood on the Altar (02195)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-05-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02195>

- For the purposes of [skull] effects in Blood on the Altar, attachments aren’t considered “underneath” other cards, and cards placed "underneath" locations because of the scenario setup instructions aren't considered attachments.
