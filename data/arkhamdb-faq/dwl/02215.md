# Key to the Chamber (02215)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02215>

- If the investigator who controls the [Key to the Chamber](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02215) is defeated, the key is discarded into the encounter discard pile. The players will have to dig through the encounter deck to find it once more.
