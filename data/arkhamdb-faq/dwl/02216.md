# Silas Bishop (02216)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02216>

- If an enemy is “considered” engaged with you because of the Massive keyword, it will enable abilities – like the [Machete](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01020)’s – that require you to be engaged with an enemy. However, there is no timing point of engagement, you’re just “considered” engaged with it, so you can’t really trigger reactions to becoming engaged with the enemy, like [Zoey Samaras](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02001) or [Zoey's Cross](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02006).
