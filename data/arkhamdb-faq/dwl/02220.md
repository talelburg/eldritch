# Kidnapped! (02220)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02220>

- The “instead” is meant to replace the rest of the “if you fail” effects (adding an ally to the pool of sacrifices and attaching Kidnapped to the current agenda), not the test itself. If you have no ally when you draw Kidnapped, you’ll still take the test, and take 2 damage if you fail.
