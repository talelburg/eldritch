# Dark Horse (02234)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-07-08; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02234>

- In response to "Can you boost a stat that isn't being tested?": "There is nothing stopping you from using [Scrapper](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02193) or any other similar asset during a test of another type (i.e. it doesn't say 'During an agility test' or anything like that). You may use it during a willpower or intellect test if you wish; you simply won't get any benefit from it besides from Dark Horse as you suggest."

- **Q:** If an investigator has [Dark Horse](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02234) in play, and one resource, then fails a skill test by 3, can they play [Lucky!](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01080) and succeed? **A:** Yes. If you spend your last resource on Lucky! while you have Dark Horse active, you can get +2 to your skill value from Lucky! and essentially +1 from Dark Horse, because Dark Horse is a constant ability. (Rules Question Form, June 2023)
