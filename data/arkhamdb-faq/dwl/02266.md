# Ace in the Hole (02266)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-15; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02266>

- [Ace in the Hole](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02266) allows you to take 3 additional actions during the turn on which it is played. It doesn't interrupt what you are doing to perform those 3 actions. If you play it during a [player [lightning] window] during a skill test, it just means that you have more actions to take during that turn. It doesn't mean that all 3 of those actions take place during that skill test.
