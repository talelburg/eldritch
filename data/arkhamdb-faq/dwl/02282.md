# Base of the Hill (02282)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-15; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02282>

- The limit on the **investigate** action on [Base of the Hill](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02282) is a limit on the number of times the ability may be initiated during the designated period [See RR, page 14 on [limits](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Limits_and_Maximums.md): "Each instance of an ability with such a limit may be initiated X times during the designated period.") So even if you fail, you cannot initiate that ability again during that round.
