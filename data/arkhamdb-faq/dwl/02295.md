# Crazed Shoggoth (02295)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-15; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02295>

- When [Crazed Shoggoth](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02295) attacks you, it creates a lasting effect that causes you to become killed instead of defeated if the shoggoth's attack defeats you. In order for this to happen, it must be the shoggoth's attack that defeats you, not another effect or ability (such as from [Hypochondria](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01100)). If something explicitly adds to the damage dealt by the attack, that would count as part of the Crazed Shoggoth's attack.
