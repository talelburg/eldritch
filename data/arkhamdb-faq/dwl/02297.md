# Spaces Between (02297)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-15; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02297>

- Locations do not leave play when they flip from revealed to unrevealed (even if the title of the card is different). That means that all tokens and attached cards will remain when they are flipped over. Technically this does allow you to tell which location is which when they are ‘shuffled’, whether that's because doom is in play due to [Destroyed Path](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02287) or if an [Obscuring Fog](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01168) is in play, but for an added challenge and mystery (and for the sake of simplicity), players can place those tokens or attachments onto a random location after they are shuffled, so that they don’t know which location is which.
