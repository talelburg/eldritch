# Chicago Typewriter (02304)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-04-30; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/02304>

- **Q:** If activating [Chicago Typewriter](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02304) costs an additional action (for example, due to [Frozen in Fear](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01164)), does that additional action give the attack +2[combat]? **A:** Yes, any and all actions spent to activate the fight ability on Chicago Typewriter count for its bonus, regardless of whether those additional actions are spent from its ability, or from some other condition (like Frozen in Fear). (May 2021)
