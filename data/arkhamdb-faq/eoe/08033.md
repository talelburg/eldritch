# Archive of Conduits (08033)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-03-16; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08033>

- **Q:** [Archive of Conduits](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08033) reads: "As an additional cost to play this asset, place 1 resource on 4 different locations, as leylines". Do these 4 resources come from your own pool or from the general token pool? **A:** The resources used as leylines for Archive of Conduits should be taken from the general token pool.
