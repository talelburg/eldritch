# Prophesiae Profana (08045)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-09-12; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08045>

- **Q:** If I play [Prophesiae Profana](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08045) and elect *not* to trigger its reaction ability (i.e., I do not choose any revealed location as "the locus"), will I therefore have +1 [intellect], +1 [agility], and the ability to ignore attacks of opportunity (or, if using Taboo, the first attack of opportunity each round) no matter what location I happen to be at (as long as Prophesiae Profana is in play, of course)? **A:** We rule that you can opt against declaring a revealed location as “the locus” during your game; in doing so, you will never be “at the locus” and you will always receive the listed bonuses while “not at the locus.”

- **Q:** If I do choose a location as "the locus" after playing [Prophesiae Profana](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08045), but then that location leaves play (e.g., is removed from the game via scenario effects), will I thereafter have +1 [intellect], +1 [agility], and the ability to ignore attacks of opportunity (or, if using Taboo, the first attack of opportunity each round) no matter what location I am at (as long as Prophesiae Profana is in play, of course)? **A:** Similarly, if a location were deemed “the locus” and it left play, you would then perpetually be considered “not at the locus.”
