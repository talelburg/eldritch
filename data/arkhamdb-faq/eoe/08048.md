# 21 or Bust (08048)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-01-02; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08048>

- **Q:** What happens if I play [21 or Bust](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08048) and the combined value of the revealed tokens is more than 21? **A:** Nothing (you bust). -  *Edge of the Earth* Investigator Expansion Frequently Asked Questions section

- **Q:** What happens if I reveal a frost ([frost]), bless ([bless]), or curse ([curse]) token while resolving [21 or Bust](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08048)? **A:** These tokens have no value when revealed outside of a skill test, so they do not add to your total. You may choose to either continue to reveal tokens or stop, as instructed. -  *Edge of the Earth* Investigator Expansion Frequently Asked Questions section
