# Close the Circle (08062)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-01-21; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08062>

- **Q:** Do neutral cards count as a class for the purposes of ***Synergy*** cards? **A:** No. Neutral is not a class; it indicates a card that does not have a class. - *Edge of the Earth* Investigator Expansion Frequently Asked Questions section

- **Q:** What is a "basic action" for the purposes of [Close the Circle](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08062)? **A:** A basic action is one that is not modified by a bold action designator on a card. For example, a basic fight action would be the standard fight action that any investigator can perform, whereas playing a [Sweeping Kick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08023) or activating the fight ability on [Dragon Pole](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08060) would both be considered non-basic fight actions. - *Edge of the Earth* Investigator Expansion Frequently Asked Questions section

- **Q:** If I use Close the Circle to perform a basic Move action, then trigger [Track Shoes](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05036) off that Move action, did Track Shoes trigger sufficiently "during" that Move action to be considered a Will test rather than a Agility test? Normally Track Shoes would trigger "after" the move action and would probably not be converted into a Will test, but it has that "but before enemies engage you" clause that makes its timing fuzzy. **A:** Yes. If you use the ability on Close the Circle, then move, you can use the ability on Track Shoes and test Willpower instead of Agility. (Rules Form, January 2024)
