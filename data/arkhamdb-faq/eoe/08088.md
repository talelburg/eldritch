# Old Shotgun (08088)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-01-02; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08088>

- **Q:** If I play [Old Shotgun](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08088) and then play an event, does it gain ammo? **A:** No. The uses keyword only affects the number of uses a card enters play with (unless an event directly references the uses keyword). In other words, if you play Old Shotgun while resolving an event (such as [Ever Vigilant](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03023) or [Sleight of Hand](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03029)), it would enter play with 2 uses, but otherwise enters play with 0. - *Edge of the Earth* Investigator Expansion Frequently Asked Questions section
