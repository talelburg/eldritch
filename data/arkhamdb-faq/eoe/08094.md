# Sledgehammer (08094)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-09-01; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08094>

- **Q:** When a card such as [Grappling Hook](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09009) or [Sledgehammer](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08094) instructs me to perform a different number of actions than the cost of the ability, how do I determine how many actions I have performed for the purposes of card effects? **A:** When resolving an ability, the investigator is considered to have performed as many actions as specified by the effect. For example, Grappling Hook or [Discipline (Balance of Body)](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08014a) instruct you to perform 3 different actions. Regardless of the cost paid to initiate the ability, you have performed 3 actions (assuming you took each available action). Conversely, an investigator activating the second ability on Sledgehammer has only performed one action, although they spent two actions to do so. - FAQ, v.2.1, August 2023
