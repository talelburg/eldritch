Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-03-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105>

- **Q:** I would like you to clarify for me the term 'using' [This question came in French, was responded to via Google Translate, and addresses multiple different cards. See: [Boxing Gloves](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60105), [Ice Pick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105), [Spirit Athame](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035), [Counterspell](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04110) - Frank]. For example, if I exhaust [Ice Pick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105) during an attack on a [Conglomeration of Spheres](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02103), do I have to discard Ice Pick?  **A:** No, you do not need to discard Ice Pick when attacking Conglomeration of Spheres. The ability on Conglomeration of Spheres refers to a card used to initiate an attack, like the **Fight** ability on Machete—Ice Pick does not have this.
