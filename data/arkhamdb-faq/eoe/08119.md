# Earthly Serenity (08119)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-09-12; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/08119>

- **Q:** Regarding [Earthly Serenity](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08117), if I succeed by 2, can I spend 2 charges to heal damage/horror to different investigators at my location? For example, heal me for 1 damage and heal my teammate for 1 horror? **A:** Yes. Earthly Serenity (1) specifies that “for each point” you succeed by, you may spend 1 to heal 1 from an investigator. “For each” allows you to choose different targets to heal 1 from.
