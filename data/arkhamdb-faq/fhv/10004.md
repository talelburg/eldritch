# Kate Winthrop (10004)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-02-25; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10004>

- **Q:** As [Kate Winthrop](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10004), can I spend clues that I place on my assets? **A:** Yes. Kate Winthrop controls all clues placed on assets she controls, and may spend or remove those clues just like any other investigator. For example, she can spend clues on her assets to advance the Act deck, or place clues from those assets on her location when she reveals he signature weakness, [Failed Experiment](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10008). Keep in mind that due to Kate's Forced ability, when an asset you control with clues on it leaves play, you must place those clues on your location. (Feast of Hemlock Vale FAQ, February 2024)
