# Eyes of Valusia (10035)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-02-28; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10035>

- **Q:** When swapping [Eyes of Valusia](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10035) with [Blade of Yoth](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10036), does it briefly take up 2 arcane and 2 hand slots? **A:** Eyes of Valusia is swapped simultaneously with Blade of Yoth. Throughout this process, they fill the same arcane and hand slots. (Feast of Hemlock Vale FAQ, February 2024)
