# Testing Sprint (10047)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2026-02-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10047>

- **Q:** When playing Testing Sprint, do I stop investigating when I fail one time, or when I finish succeeding or failing to investigate each eligible location? **A:** The former. If you fail one investigate test before you’ve investigated each eligible location, you do not continue investigating other locations. (Official FAQ v2.5, February 2026)
