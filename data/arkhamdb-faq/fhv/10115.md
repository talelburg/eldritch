# Wrong Place, Right Time (10115)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-03-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10115>

- **Q:** If a card ability allows me to move damage/horror to another card, can I move that damage/horror to a card without a health or sanity value? **A:** No. An effect that can “move” damage/horror from one card to another—such as [Wrong Place, Right Time](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10115)—must move that damage/horror to a card that has a health or sanity value, respectively.
