# Eldritch Tongue (10128)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-03-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10128>

- **Q:** Can you use [Eldritch Tongue](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10128) to play [Snitch](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10078) from your discard pile? **A:** Yes, you can play Snitch from your discard pile with Eldritch Tongue. (January 2024)

- **Q:** If I use [Eldritch Tongue](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10128) to play an event that attaches to another card, such as [Beguile](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10010), when does that event get removed from the game? **A:** An event that attaches to another card is considered “resolved” when all abilities and effects triggered by it entering play resolve, including its attachment effect. If you play Beguile using Eldritch Tongue, Beguile will attach to the enemy, then immediately get removed from the game. (FAQ 2.3, October 2024)
