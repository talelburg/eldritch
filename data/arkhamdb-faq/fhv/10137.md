# The Silver Moth (10137)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-03-27; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10137>

- **Q:** Imagine an investigator has [The Silver Moth](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10137) in play, occupying their 1 accessory slot. They attempt to play [Holy Rosary](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01059). In the above situation, does the investigator ends up with both Holy Rosary and The Silver Moth in play? **A:** The Silver Moth’s ability prevents its bearer from playing any new items into their accessory slot, meaning they could not attempt to play Holy Rosary while they had The Silver Moth in play. (March 2024)
