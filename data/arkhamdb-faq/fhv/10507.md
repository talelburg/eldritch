# Mine Cart (10507)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-06-07; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/10507>

- **Q:** Does intention of ability of this card: "[reaction] When Mine Cart moves, spend 1 switch: Either cancel the move, or move an additional time" is that you decide do you want to move additional time before you move (before you will reveal the location) or you decide to move additional time after you move (after you reveal location). In this case ability should read: "when mine cart move spend 1 switch to cancel the move, or after mine cart move spend 1 switch to move additional time." **A:** If you wish to trigger the reaction ability on Mine Cart, you must choose whether or not to spend a switch before Mine Cart moves and before the location it moves to is revealed. (Ruling, April 2025)
