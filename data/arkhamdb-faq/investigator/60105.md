# Boxing Gloves (60105)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-03-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/60105>

- **Q:** I would like you to clarify for me the term 'using' [This question came in French, was responded to via Google Translate, and addresses multiple different cards. See: [Boxing Gloves](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60105), [Ice Pick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105), [Spirit Athame](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035), [Counterspell](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04110) - Frank]. For example, if I have [Boxing Gloves](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60105) when I attack, does that mean I use the gloves? I would like to check if we could enchant the gloves (using [Enchant Weapon](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07261) with the gloves?) **A:** No; making an attack while having Boxing Gloves in play does not count as “using” the Gloves. The [reaction] on Enchant Weapon only adds Willpower to your attack when you’re using/activating a weapon with a Fight bold action designator, like Machete.
