# Miskatonic Archaeology Funding (60232)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-10-06; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/60232>

- **Q:** Does the forced ability of [Miskatonic Archaeology Funding](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60232)  mean 1 damage for each ***Miskatonic*** ally or 1 damage in total? **A:** With [Miskatonic Archaeology Funding](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60232), you can assign 1 damage/horror in total to a ***Miskatonic*** asset per instance of damage/horror. You can’t assign to multiple ***Miskatonic*** assets. (September 2023)
