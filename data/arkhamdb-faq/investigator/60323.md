# Cheap Shot (60323)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2020-10-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/60323>

- **Q:** If a card such as [Cheap Shot](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60323) is returned to my hand, what areas am I allowed to retrieve it from? If it is shuffled into my deck, can I still return it to my hand? **A:** In general, unless otherwise specified, players can “return” such a card from any play area, so long as it is in an area that allows the card to be found and identified. For example, if Cheap Shot is in the discard pile or attached to another card (such as a [Crystallizer of Dreams](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06024)) and you are able to find it, you may return it to your hand from that area. However, if it is in a place where its position is impossible to determine (such as shuffled into your deck) or facedown in a place where you are not allowed to look at its other side (for example, as a swarm card), you would be unable to return it to your hand, and therefore that aspect of the effect would fail. Additionally, if Cheap Shot is removed from the game, unless specifically stated otherwise, no game effects can interact with it in any way until the
end of the game. - FAQ, v.1.8, October 2020
