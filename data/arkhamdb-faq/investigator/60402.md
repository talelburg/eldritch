# Arbiter of Fates (60402)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-09-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/60402>

- **Q:** Assuming [Arbiter of Fates](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60402) is in play, can I trigger [Jacqueline Fine](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60401)'s reaction triggered ability twice in one skill test? **A:** No. Each reaction triggered ability may be triggered only once each time the specified condition on the ability is met, and cannot react to itself. Jacqueline cannot trigger her ability a second time when revealing tokens, be it for a generic skill test or while resolving Dark Future. (September 2023)
