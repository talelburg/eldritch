# Heirloom of Hyperborea (90018)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-09-12; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/90018>

- **Q:** Regarding [Heirloom of Hyperborea](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01012), the text reads "After you play a Spell card: Draw 1 card". When I play [Read the Signs](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06117), when can I trigger the [reaction] trigger ability of the heirloom to draw 1 card? Does it trigger when I play this card, or after I completely resolve Read the Signs and discard this card? **A:** Yes, the Heirloom of Hyperborea can trigger off of any Spell card its controller plays. This trigger occurs after the Spell card has resolved in full.
