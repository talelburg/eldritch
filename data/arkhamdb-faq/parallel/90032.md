# By the Book (90032)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-11-21; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/90032>

- **Q:** In the scenario guide, it says that Roland's XP is based on enemies in the victory display, and other investigators get XP from locations. What happens to other XP in the display from things like [Delve Too Deep](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02111) and [Bury Them Deep](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03016)? Is this lost? Or should XP Bonuses from player cards still be added to all players' XP? **A:** Those cards are ignored when awarding XP. The resolution text of a rulebook must state if and how the investigators earn XP when completing a scenario. In most scenarios, investigators earn XP based on each card in the victory display (e.g. “Each investigator earns experience equal to the Victory X value of each card in the victory display.”) By The Book instead awards XP specifically based on the enemies in the victory display and the locations in the victory display. So, the Victory X keyword on [Delve Too Deep](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02111) and [Bury Them Deep](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03016) is ignored while awarding XP at the end of the scenario. (October 2023)
