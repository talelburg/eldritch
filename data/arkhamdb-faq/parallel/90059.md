# Zoey Samaras (90059)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-02-06; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/90059>

- **Q:** If [Zoey Samaras](https://arkhamdb.com/card/90059) activates her [fast] "Before you reveal chaos tokens during an attack, remove 3 [bless] tokens from the chaos bag: This attack deals +1 damage. (Limit once per round.)" and then reacts with [Nephthys](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07262) [reaction] "When 1 or more [bless] tokens would be removed from the chaos bag during a skill test: Seal them on Nephthys, instead.", have the removed tokens still been removed to satisfy the cost of Zoey? **A:** No. The ability on Nephthys replaces the removal of tokens from the chaos bag with sealing them; if you use it, you will not satisfy the cost on Parallel Zoey’s free triggered ability. (February 2024)
