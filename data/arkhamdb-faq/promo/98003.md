# Sacrificial Beast (98003)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-02-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/98003>

- **Q:** [Sacrificial Beast](https://arkhamdb.com/card/98003) indicates Jenny cannot gain resources through card effects. Is Jenny's own effect of collecting an additional resource in upkeep considered gaining resources through a card effect for this purpose, or does the fact that it's an additional resource added to resources gained via a normal game procedure mean it is not actually gaining resources through a card effect? **A:** "Yes, the additional resource collected from Jenny’s ability is still a resource gained via a card effect, so while Sacrificial Beast is in play, you will not collect the additional resource from Jenny’s ability."
