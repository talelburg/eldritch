# Akachi Onyele (03004)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-07-19; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03004>

- **Q:** Since the text of Pendant of the Queen has "uses (charges)", can [Akachi Onyele](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03004) purchase [Segment of Onyx](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06021)? **A:** ~~Yes, Akachi can purchase Segment of Onyx.~~ Bonded cards **cannot** be treated as part of the text box of the card to which they are bonded. (August 2023)

- **Q:**  I have a question regarding Akachi's ability. It says "Your assets with "uses (charges)" enter play with 1 additional charge on them". However, what exactly is "your asset"? The rules say that "your", among other things, refers to "investigator who controls the card". So, here's the question. How does she interact with abilities that put cards under her control? 1. For example, [Living Ink](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09079) with Shifting Ink upgrade says "You may play Living Ink under the control of another investigator at your location". If, say, Luke plays Living Ink on Akachi, does it get an extra charge? 2. Same situation, but now Akachi is the one who plays Living Ink on Luke, does it get an extra charge? 3. Do Akachi's items played by [Bob Jenkins](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08016) get an extra charge? 4. Do cards she plays via [Black Market](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08055) from another player's deck get an extra charge? Thank you in advance. **A:** For Akachi’s ability, any assets played under her control with “uses (charges)” gains 1 additional charge, regardless of if she owns that asset. If she plays such an asset under someone else’s control, it does not get 1 additional charge. Bob’s ability plays the asset under her control, so it gets 1 additional charge; similarly, as long as the asset from Black Market is played under her control, it gets 1 additional charge. (Rules Form, July 2025)
