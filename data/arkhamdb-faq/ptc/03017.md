# Graveyard Ghouls (03017)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-09-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03017>

- What happens if the Yorick player has [Graveyard Ghouls](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03017) engaged with him or her and runs out of deck? "If an investigator with an empty investigator deck needs to draw a card, that investigator shuffles his or her discard pile back into his or her deck, then draws the card, and upon completion of the entire draw takes one horror. In this case, you cannot shuffle your discard pile into your deck, so you will neither draw 1 card, nor will you take 1 horror."
