# Crisis of Identity (03019)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-09-26; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03019>

- Generally speaking, card effects only interact with cards that are in play. Cards that are out of play aren’t typically affected by card effects unless explicitly stated. So, even though you control the cards in your hand and deck, [Crisis of Identity](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03019) only ever discards cards from play, because it does not specify that it discards cards from your hand or deck.
