# Fieldwork (03024)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-07-03; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03024>

- **Q:** If I move during a skill test (by, for instance, triggering the [reaction] on [Fieldwork](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03024)) and trigger [Fieldwork](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03024), does "next skill test" mean the next test I resolve or the next test I initiate. 
**A:** (the latter:) If you move during a skill test, Fieldwork will not grant a bonus for that skill test, it will grant a bonus for the next skill test you initiate.
