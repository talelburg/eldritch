# Astral Travel (03034)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-11-09; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03034>

- **Q:** [Robes of Endless Night](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60422) has a [reaction] triggered ability that makes it so playing a ***Spell*** card does not provoke an attack of opportunity. If I used this ability in reaction to playing [Astral Travel](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03034), which has a bold "Move" keyword, would I provoke an attack of opportunity? That is, does the ability on Robes merely prevent attacks of opportunity from taking a Play action to play Spell cards while not preventing attacks of opportunity from the card's effects, or does it cover the entire playing of the card, so to speak? **A:** To answer your question(s):

 - Exhausting Robes of Endless Night (2) when you play Astral Travel would not result in an Attack of Opportunity.
 - Also noting that the “play” action taken by playing Astral Travel would only result in one AoO; a second one for Astral Travel’s Move would not occur. MJ described this as “a play action that was also a move action.”
