# Spirit Athame (03035)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-03-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035>

- **Q:** What consistitutes a "skill test on a ***Spell*** card" for the purposes of cards like [Spirit Athame](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035) and [Grounded](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03113)? **A:** A "skill test on a card" is any ability that directly prompts a skill test, either
through the template "test skill (X)," or by initiating an action that is, in itself, a skill test (for example, any card with **Fight**, **Evade**, or **Investigate** action designators). - FAQ v1.2, Jan 2018.

- **Q:** I would like you to clarify for me the term 'using' [This question came in French, was responded to via Google Translate, and addresses multiple different cards. See: [Boxing Gloves](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60105), [Ice Pick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105), [Spirit Athame](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035), [Counterspell](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04110) - Frank]. For example, if I exhaust [Spirit Athame](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035) during an attack with a **Spell** on a [Conglomeration of Spheres](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02103), do I have to discard Spirit Athame? **A:** If you exhaust Spirit Athame during a skill test on a Spell card (like [Shrivelling](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01060)), then no, you do not need to discard it when attacking Conglomeration of Spheres. You only discard Spirit Athame if you used/activated its **Fight** ability against Conglomeration of Spheres.
