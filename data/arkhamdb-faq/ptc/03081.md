# Dianne Devine (03081)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-09-26; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03081>

- Both [The Rougarou](https://arkhamdb.com/card/81028) and [Dianne Devine](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03081) instruct you to “find” the destination first, which could indeed be the same location they’re already in. There is a slight difference in the way their move effects are worded, though; The Rougarou reads “Move The Rougarou (one location at a time) until it enters that location,” whereas Dianne reads “Move Dianne Devine to that asset’s location, if able.” The Rougarou therefore must still move (in some cases out and then back into the same location), whereas Dianne will simply remain where she is if the Bystander asset that you “found" was already at her location.
