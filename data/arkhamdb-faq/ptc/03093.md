# Poltergeist (03093)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-11-01; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03093>

- Can I use [The Home Front](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03007) to 'move' a damage onto [Poltergeist](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03093), given it says 'cannot be damaged'? 
No: "To be or to become damaged would include anything that would place, add, or move damage tokens onto the card."
