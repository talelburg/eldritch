# Grounded (03113)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-01-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03113>

- **Q:** What consistitutes a "skill test on a ***Spell*** card" for the purposes of cards like [Spirit Athame](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03035) and [Grounded](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03113)? **A:** A "skill test on a card" is any ability that directly prompts a skill test, either
through the template "test skill (X)," or by initiating an action that is, in itself, a skill test (for example, any card with **Fight**, **Evade**, or **Investigate** action designators). - FAQ v1.2, Jan 2018.
