# Clasp of Black Onyx (03142)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-07-11; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03142>

- **Q:** Can you discard [Clasp of Black Onyx](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03142) to [Corrosion](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03102) or [Realm of Madness](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03338) if you can't reach the discard threshold with other cards in hand? **A:** No, in this case, Corrosion would not discard the Clasp, because weaknesses cannot be discarded from your hand except when it is at random. Even if there are no other options for Corrosion to discard, the Clasp would remain in your hand.
