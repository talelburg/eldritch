# Patient Confinement (03178)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-01-02; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03178>

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata from the Arkham Horror FAQ in its card text, so the ArkhamDB text and the card image above differ, as the ArkhamDB text has been edited to contain this erratum (updated January 2022): **Erratum:** Each of the Patient Confinement locations should not have the ***Arkham Asylum*** trait. - FAQ v1.2, Jan 2018.

- 'Ignore the text on the unrevealed side of ***Arkham Asylum*** locations': this appears on ["The Really Bad Ones" (v. I)](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03164), ["The Really Bad Ones" (v. II)](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03165), [Planning the Escape](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03166) and [No Asylum](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03167). As written, it means entering the Patient Confinement cells ([1](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03178), [2](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03179), [3](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03180) and [4](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03181)) does not require a clue to be spent. Matt writes: "This was unintentional and we are looking into fixing this, perhaps in the next edition of the FAQ."
