# Straitjacket (03185)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-07-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03185>

- [Straitjacket](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03185) vs [The King in Yellow](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03011) or [The Necronomicon](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01009): if you have Straitjacket in play and draw either of the signature weaknesses that currently take up hand slots, or vice versa, the revelation effect is completed and the book is put into play. At that point, your slots are 'overloaded' but 'cannot' is absolute: Straitjacket 'cannot leave play' except by [action][action], and the signature weaknesses 'cannot leave play' except by effects described on the cards. So both remain in play.

- **Q:** Can I activate the [action][action] ability on a copy of Straitjacket that is in another player’s threat area? **A:** Yes. Even though it is being treated as an asset, it is still a treachery in your threat area and other players may trigger its [action][action] ability. (Official FAQ v2.4, July 2025)
