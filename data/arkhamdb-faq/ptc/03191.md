# Logical Reasoning (03191)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-01-08; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03191>

- **Q:** can [Logical Reasoning](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03191) be used to discard a Hidden card with the ***Terror*** trait? **A:** Hidden cards cannot be discarded by any means save for the ability on the card itself. That means they cannot be discarded via Logical Reasoning, even though they might otherwise be able to be discarded (since they are in fact a Terror card considered to be in your threat area).
