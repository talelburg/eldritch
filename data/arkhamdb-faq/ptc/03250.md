# Bone-Filled Caverns (03250)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-03-27; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03250>

- **Q:** If I am holding two copies of [Magnifying Glass](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01030) while investigating [Bone-Filled Caverns](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03250), am I forced to discard one of them? If I am holding a [Flamethrower](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04305) while investigating Bone-Filled Cavern, am I forced to discard it? **A:** If you investigate Bone-Filled Cavern and have 2 hand slots filled with assets, you must discard assets until you have 1 or 0 hand slots filled instead. In the first example, you’d discard one Magnifying Glass; in the second, you’d discard the Flamethrower. (March 2024)
