# Ward of Protection (03270)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2020-01-15; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03270>

- **Q:** Can I play [Dark Insight](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05014) or [Ward of Protection II](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03270) to cancel another player's treachery if it has peril? **A:** You cannot play cards or use any reaction abilities when somebody else is resolving a peril card, so you would not be able to play Dark Insight [or Ward II] to cancel it.
