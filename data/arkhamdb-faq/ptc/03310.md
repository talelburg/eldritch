# Cheat Death (03310)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-02-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/03310>

- **Q:** Can I use [Cheat Death](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03310) to avoid being defeated by ["I'll see you in hell!"](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03189)? **A:** Cheat Death does not actually cancel being defeated or replace being defeated; it simply provides you with a means of avoiding defeat (typically through damage or horror). Card effects that simply defeat you, such as “I’ll see you in hell”, will still resolve and will still cause you to be defeated.
