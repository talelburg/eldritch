# Dark Bidding (51023)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-09-01; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/51023>

- **Q:** When I draw [Dark Bidding](https://arkhamdb.com/card/51023), it instructs me to attach to the [Hunting Horror](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02141) even when out of play. Is this 'out of play' reference intended to only apply when the Hunting Horror is in the Void or is it also supposed to attach while in the encounter deck? **A:** Dark Bidding would attach to Hunting Horror while it’s in the void. It would not be able to attach to Hunting Horror if Hunting Horror were in a deck or discard pile.
