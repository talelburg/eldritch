# Curse of the Rougarou (81029)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/81029>

- When [Curse of the Rougarou](https://arkhamdb.com/card/81029) is 'put into play' in the lead investigator's threat area, 'put into play' means essentially the same thing as 'add'. After the scenario, the curse is added to that investigator's deck for the remainder of the campaign.
