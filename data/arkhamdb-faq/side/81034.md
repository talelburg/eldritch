# On the Prowl (81034)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-01-06; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/81034>

- If The Rougarou is in a location that is tied for the fewest clues on it, you may choose to 'find' that location, move The Rougarou to a connecting location, then move it back to where it was. Note that if it's [Act 2](https://arkhamdb.com/card/81006), this will make The Rougarou drop two clues.
