# The Festivities Begin (82002)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-02-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/82002>

- For Baleful Reveler's **Forced** ability, players may choose any investigator to reveal the token. The chosen investigator can use abilities that refer to \*you* revealing a token ([Wendy](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01005), [Jim](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02004), [Grotesque Statue](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01071)).

- Special effects of revealed tokens described on the scenario refrerence card do not resolve.
