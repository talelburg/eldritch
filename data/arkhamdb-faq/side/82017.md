# Don Lagorio (82017)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2017-07-10; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/82017>

- When calculating the shortest route to investigators, [Don Lagorio](https://arkhamdb.com/card/82017) will only move counter-clockwise if the nearest investigator is in the counter-clockwise location. If investigators are two locations counter-clockwise from Don Lagorio, the shortest route for him is still going clockwise through the Venice locations.
