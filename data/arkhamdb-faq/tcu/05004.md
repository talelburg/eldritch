# Diana Stanley (05004)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-08-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004>

- **Q:** Does cancelling or ignoring a chaos token count as a cancel/ignore for [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004)'s [reaction] ability? **A:** A chaos token is a game effect which can be ignored or canceled. If you cancel or ignore a chaos token using one of your cards, you can place that card beneath Diana Stanley using her reaction ability. This includes both [Counterspell](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04110) and [Dark Prophecy](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04032), as well as any other card effect that cancels or ignores a chaos token or a chaos token’s effect (such as [Defiance](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02190), [Grotesque Statue](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01071), etc).

- **Q:** I have a question concerning [Sefina Rousseau](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03003) and [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004). Are cards put beneath these investigators by their abilities considered to be in play, or out of play? The question arises because they're physically in an investigator's play area, which suggests they might be in play, but there are a few implications that makes me want to confirm. For example, if they are in play, that implies that a treachery such as [Ants!](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04221) can discard them, which feels strange. Can you confirm if those cards are in play or not? **A:** These cards are considered out of play. They cannot be affected by anything other than the abilities that refer to them explicitly (like Sefina's action ability, [Twilight Blade](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05013), etc).

- **Q:** Can I look at facedown cards beneath or attached to other cards I control? If I use the [fast] ability on [Ancestral Knowledge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303) to draw one of the cards attached to it, am I allowed to know what each card is, or is it random? **A:** Unless an effect states otherwise, investigators are allowed to look at the other sides of facedown cards beneath or attached to cards they control, such as [Ancestral Knowledge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303), [Backpack](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04037), and [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004). If you are given the ability to draw, play, or interact with one of those cards,
you may look at them to know which one you wish to draw or play. This is not true for facedown cards that are attached to encounter cards or placed facedown via a scenario effect, such as swarm cards, empty space, or tomes beneath locations in the challenge scenario Read or Die. In these instances, players cannot look at the other side of those cards unless instructed otherwise. - FAQ, v.1.9, June 2021

- **Q:** If [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004) has <5 cards beneath her, can she use an cancel/ignore effect on a permanent asset such as [Sacred Covenant](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07110) to trigger her [reaction] ability to draw a card and gain a resource effectively every turn? **A:** Yeah, it appears so! Diana’s effect doesn’t use the word “then,” so there is no requirement that the card be placed beneath her in order to get the card and resource. And Sacred Covenant surely does ignore a game effect. However, it’s worth noting that one or more [bless] tokens must actually be returned to the bag and ignored in this way in order to trigger Diana’s response. It’s Diana’s blessed day!

- [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004)’s [reaction] ability typically interacts with events in limbo or other cards that may or may not be in play. As such, Diana Stanley’s ability can be triggered even if that card is currently in limbo or in the discard pile. - FAQ, v.2.0, August 2022, Card Ability Interpretation 2.22, see [Limbo](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Limbo.md).
