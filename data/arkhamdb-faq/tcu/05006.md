# Marie Lambeau (05006)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-09-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/05006>

- **Q:** Does [Marie Lambeau](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05006)'s Deckbuilding Options ***Spell*** traits work with bonded cards? For example, can Marie upgrade into [Occult Lexicon](https://arkhamdb.com/card/54004) (3)? **A:** ~~Yes, Marie can upgrade Occult Lexicon.~~ Bonded cards **cannot** be treated as part of the text box of the card to which they are bonded. (August 2023)
