# Family Inheritance (05011)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2021-08-25; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/05011>

- **Q:** If [Preston](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05003) draws [Paranoia](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01097) during his turn, are the resources on [Family Inheritance](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05011) discarded? **A:** Paranoia only gets rid of resources that are in your resource pool.

- **"As If":** This was added to the FAQ (v.1.7, March 2020) and then amended (v.1.8, October 2020). You can read the October ruling on the ArkhamDB rules page [here](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/As_If.md).
