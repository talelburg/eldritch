# Terrible Secret (05015)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-01-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/05015>

- **Q:** How does [Terrible Secret](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05015) interact with cards like [Ward of Protection](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01065) (cancel), [Delay the Inevitable](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05021) (horror cancellation) and [Deny Existence](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05032) (horror ignoring) **A:** Terrible Secret reads: "Cannot be canceled." at the end of its effect. This means that any card effect which would cancel the card or any of its effects would fail (such as Delay the Inevitable). However, card effects which ignore the horror, such as Deny Existence, would still work, because it is not cancelling anything.

- **Q:** If I draw [Terrible Secret](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05015) with five cards beneath [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004) and I do not choose to discard these cards, but choose to take 5 Horror, how much of it can I ignore when I play [Deny Existence](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05032)? **A:** For Terrible Secret, you may only use Deny Existence on one of the choices, essentially ignoring 1 of the horror you would take. (The card presents a choice for each card under Diana, so it “cannot be resolved simultaneously.”)
