# Enchanted Blade (05118)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2019-06-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/05118>

- **Q:** Do the basic +1 [combat] and the second +1 [combat] when you empower the blade combine for +2 [combat]? **A:** Yes, the two bonuses stack, because it’s not a replacement effect (it doesn’t say ‘instead’). So regular Enchanted Blade indeed gives you +2 fight and +1 damage if you spend one of its charges.
