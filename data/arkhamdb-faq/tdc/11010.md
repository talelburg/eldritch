# Glimpse the Void (11010)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2026-02-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11010>

- **Q:** How does Glimpse the Void interact with other abilities that reveal tokens? **A:** Glimpse the Void causes you to reveal and resolve 1 additional token on top of any you would normally reveal and resolve by/for the test or effect. This token is targeted, chosen, or ignored separately from the other token(s) revealed by/for the test or effect. (Official FAQ v2.5, February 2026)
