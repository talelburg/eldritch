# Cast Adrift (11019)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-07-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11019>

- **Q:** If I’m playing [George Barnaby](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11017) and I draw [Cast Adrift](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11019) during the upkeep phase, when and how is my hand size calculated? **A:** George Barnaby’s first ability is a constant ability. If the number of cards beneath George Barnaby changes, your maximum hand size immediately changes to the new number. If you draw Cast Adrift during step 4.4 of the upkeep phase, you immediately discard each card beneath George Barnaby, which sets your maximum hand size to 0. You can then trigger George’s [reaction] ability to place 1 discarded card beneath him and draw 1 card, setting your maximum hand size to 1. Then at step 4.5 of the upkeep phase, your hand size is equal to your current maximum. (Official FAQ v2.4, July 2025)
