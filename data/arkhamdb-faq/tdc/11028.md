# Bulwark (11028)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-03-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11028>

- **Q:** How often can I resolve the seal keyword on [Bulwark](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11028), [Misdirection](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11043), and [Obscure](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11060)? **A:** When you meet the triggering conditions for bulwark, Misdirection, or Obscure, and there is at least 1 chaos token of the listed type in the chaos bag, you may resolve the seal keyword on that asset. If there is no chaos token(s) of the listed type(s) in the chaos bag, then there are none to seal and you cannot resolve that keyword. You may only resolve the seal keyword once per instance of meeting its triggering condition. (The Drowned City Investigator Expansion FAQ, March 2025)
