# Cosmic Revelation (11041)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-03-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11041>

- **Q:** What happens when I use Cosmic Revelation's ability while an investigator has a card with an undefined cost (-) in their hand? **A:** Cards with an undefined cost (-) are not considered to have an even or odd cost while in hand. As such, they cannot be revealed nor played via the effect of Cosmic Revelation. (The Drowned City Investigator Expansion FAQ, March 2025)
