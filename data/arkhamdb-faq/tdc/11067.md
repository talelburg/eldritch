# Eyes of the Dreamer (11067)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2026-02-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11067>

- **Q:** If I use [Katarina Sojka](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11069)'s ability while revealing tokens with [Eyes of the Dreamer](https://arkhamdb.com/card/11067), does Katarina’s ability to “ignore” unresolved tokens combo with Eyes checking “ignored” tokens for a match? **A:** Yes. A token ignored via Katarina Sojka’s ability counts as “an ignored token” for Eyes of the Dreamer’s ability. (Official FAQ v2.5, February 2026)
