# The Great Work (11068a)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2026-02-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11068a>

- **Q:** If my investigator is usurped by the Homunculus, what happens to the trauma suffered by my original investigator? **A:** Any physical and mental trauma suffered by an investigator who is usurped by the Homunculus is transferred to the Homunculus. The Homunculus begins each scenario with all trauma suffered by the investigator it has usurped. (The Drowned City Investigator Expansion FAQ, March 2025)

- **Q:** What happens if the Lost Homunculus attempts to exile a card from their deck? **A:** Exiling a card or removing a card from the game as a cost cannot be canceled or ignored. Therefore, the Lost Homunculus will successfully exile the card in question. Note that if they do so, they are not able to purchase any cards when the scenario ends, and they have no defined “legal deck size” to maintain. (Official FAQ v2.5, February 2026)
