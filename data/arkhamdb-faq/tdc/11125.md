# Memories of Another Life (11125)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2026-02-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/11125>

- **Q:** If I use Memories of Another Life to play a card that summons 1 or more bonded cards, can I summon those cards even though they were not set aside at the beginning of the game? **A:** Yes. Although bonded cards should normally be set aside when the game begins, you would set any corresponding bonded cards aside, out of play, when you play a card with Memories of Another Life. (Official FAQ v2.4, July 2025)

- Memories of Another Life can only search the collection for a chosen card. It cannot search any in-play or out-of-play area involved in the current game, and it cannot create a new copy of a card that does not exist physically within your collection. Memories of Another Life cannot search for a copy of a customizable card the committing investigator has already upgraded, as such a card is no longer level 0 for that investigator. It can play a level 0 card that has been Chained via the List of Taboos. A card played by Memories of Another life can be placed in the victory display, or any other eligible out-of-play area, by an ability or game effect. (Official FAQ v2.5, February 2026)
