# Detached from Reality (06014)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-11-21; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/06014>

- **Q:** Under [The Silver Rule](../../rules-reference/rules/The_Thing_That_Should_Not_Be.md#the-silver-rule-added-in-faq-section-card-ability-interpretation-point-220), "the encounter card takes precedence over the player card", does this include weakness with an encounter cardtype? For example, what happens if Luke is [Entombed](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04089) (or the location has [No Turning Back](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04228) attached) and he draws [Detached from Reality](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06014)? Does this mean, because of The Silver Rule, Luke cannot move into [Dream-Gate](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06015b)? **A:** Because Entombed states that the person it’s attached to cannot disengage or move, this effect is absolute and must be followed. If Luke were Entombed and drew Detached from Reality, Luke would not disengage from enemies or move, he would only either flip Dream-Gate or put Dream-Gate into play.

- **Q:**  There's a ruling that states: "I would say that while Dream-Gate is in play, it is an encounter card, since it is a location and location cards are generally encounter cards. However, while it is set aside and out of play it functions more like a player card, for deckbuilding purposes and the like. So if Gate Box moves you, that is a player card effect moving you, but when Dream-Gate moves you, that would be an encounter card effect moving you." MJ says: "While Dream-Gate is in play, it is an encounter card", does this mean that when the effect of an encounter card conflicts with the Constant Abilities of Dream-Gate, The Silver Rule will itwork? For example, during Luke's turn, Luke is in Dream-Gate (Wondrous Journey) and draws a [Dance of the Yellow King](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03097). The skill test fails, the nearest Lunatic will move towards Luke. If both cards are encounter cards, may I decide which takes precedence? If so, does the enemy stay in the same place? **A:** If Luke is in Dream-Gate, enemies cannot enter it, nor can enemies move closer to him, so Dance of the Yellow King would not move the enemy anywhere if Luke failed the [willpower] test. The Silver Rule comes into play if two cards directly contradict one another in a way that is “impossible to reconcile”, but this is not an instance where the interaction is impossible to reconcile.

- **Q:** Luke draws [Detached from Reality](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06014) in the Upkeep phase and moves to Dream-Gate (Pointless Reality). Then in the next Mythos phase, if Luke draws an enemy that has no spawn instruction, how should enemies spawn in this case? Spawns engaged with Luke? Just discard this enemy? or something else? **A:** If the enemy cannot spawn anywhere, discard it.
