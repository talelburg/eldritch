# Solemn Vow (06020)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-09-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/06020>

- **Q:** Can damage/horror be "moved" to asset cards without health/sanity  (e.g. [.45 Automatic](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01516))? If not, what about asset cards like [Relentless](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60109) that can have damage placed on it? Can the damage/horror "moved" exceed the upper limit of health/sanity of the asset card? **A:** No, damage/horror can’t be moved to asset cards without health/sanity, similar to how they can’t be dealt or assigned damage/horror. No. Relentless can only have damage placed on it in the manner described by its ability, and can’t have damage moved to it. No. A card will be defeated immediately if the damage/horror moved to it exceeds its health/sanity (August 2023).
