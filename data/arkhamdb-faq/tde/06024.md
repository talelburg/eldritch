- **Q:** How does [The Painted World](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03012) interact with [Crystallizer of Dreams](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06024), as they both replace the discard effect? **A:** "Both effects are replacement effects, and since The Painted World’s replacement effect is forced and Crystallizer of Dreams is optional, the forced effect would take priority and resolves first. Therefore The Painted World must be removed from the game after it is played and cannot be placed underneath Crystallizer of Dreams."

- **Q:** Which triggers first: Parallel [Agnes Baker](https://arkhamdb.com/card/90017) ability or [Heirloom of Hyperborea](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01012)? What about [Crystallizer of Dreams](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06024)? **A:** An event card is placed on top of its owner’s discard pile during step 4 of the play/initiation sequence, so any effect that modifies where the event goes (such as Parallel Agnes Baker or Crystallizer of Dreams) must occur during that step. An effect that triggers “after you play” an event but does not modify where the event goes (such as Heirloom of Hyberborea’s ability) would naturally trigger just after step 4. - FAQ, v.2.0, August 2022

- **Q:** If a card such as [Cheap Shot](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60323) is returned to my hand, what areas am I allowed to retrieve it from? If it is shuffled into my deck, can I still return it to my hand? **A:** In general, unless otherwise specified, players can “return” such a card from any play area, so long as it is in an area that allows the card to be found and identified. For example, if Cheap Shot is in the discard pile or attached to another card (such as a [Crystallizer of Dreams](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06024)) and you are able to find it, you may return it to your hand from that area. However, if it is in a place where its position is impossible to determine (such as shuffled into your deck) or facedown in a place where you are not allowed to look at its other side (for example, as a swarm card), you would be unable to return it to your hand, and therefore that aspect of the effect would fail. Additionally, if Cheap Shot is removed from the game, unless specifically stated otherwise, no game effects can interact with it in any way until the end of the game. - FAQ, v.1.8, October 2020
