# Easy Mark (06026)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-11-09; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/06026>

- **Q:** If [Wendy's Amulet](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01014) is already in play, suppose Wendy has only 2 [Easy Mark](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06026) left in her deck. Wendy plays 1 Easy Mark in her hand, resolves the card effect, and draws 1 card. The new Easy Mark, because the mandatory ability is placed at the bottom of the deck, can she use the [reaction] trigger ability to play this newly drawn Easy Mark]card again for free? Will it be possible to obtain "unlimited" resources? **A:** I’m not sure if the situation you’re describing is likely to happen, but in theory Wendy could continue playing Easy Mark cards to generate as many resources as desired.

- **Q:** Is [Easy Mark](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06026) normally in the discard pile when its [reaction] ability triggers? **A:** Yes, Easy Mark will normally be in the discard pile when its [reaction] Triggered Ability occurs. After using a “play” action on event cards, they are discarded. That said, you are still able to resolve its [reaction] Triggered Ability as written and play another Easy Mark if you have one.

- **Q:** Can [Easy Mark](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06026)'s [reaction] ability still trigger if it isn't in the discard pile, e.g. if [Wendy's Amulet](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01014) put it on the bottom of the deck? **A:** Yes; if Wendy’s Amulet caused Easy Mark to be put somewhere else after it was played, you could still resolve Easy Mark’s [reaction] Triggered Ability.
