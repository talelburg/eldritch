# "Fool me once..." (06156)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2026-02-22; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/06156>

- **Q:** Can you play ["Fool me once..."](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06156) if the effect you're resolving is to discard the treachery? E.g. [Frozen in Fear](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01164)'s "if you succeed, discard Frozen in Fear" or [Deep Dark](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04094) discarding at the end of the round? **A:** Yes, “Fool me once…” could be attached to those so long as you have resolved an effect on the Treachery card and would normally discard it (not “remove it from the game”, etc.)

- **Q:** Can you play "Fool me once…" if upon advancing to the next agenda counts as discarding a treachery? E.g. [Day of Reckoning](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07040) or [Light of Aforgomon](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02085)? **A:** Yes, “Fool me once…” could be attached to those so long as you have resolved an effect on the Treachery card and would normally discard it (not “remove it from the game”, etc.)

- **Q:** Can you play "Fool me once…" if the Action triggered abilities effect you're resolving is to discard the treachery weakness? E.g. [Haunted](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01098) or [Internal Injury](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02038)? **A:** Yes, “Fool me once…” could be attached to those so long as you have resolved an effect on the Treachery card and would normally discard it (not “remove it from the game”, etc.)

- **Q:** Can you play "Fool me once…" if help teammates pay Action triggered abilities effect you're resolving is to discard the treachery weakness? E.g. [Narcolepsy](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06037)? **A:** “Fool me once…” requires “you” to be the person that discards the Treachery, so if another player discarded Narcolepsy for you, “you” would not be able to use “Fool me once…” Only the person who discarded the Treachery could use “Fool me once…”

- **Q:** Are cards attached to "Fool me once…" face-up and/or in play? Since Fool me once…" attaches the treachery face up, is its effect still active? E.g. [Drawing the Sign](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03041), [Self-Destructive](https://arkhamdb.com/card/60104) or [Evil Past](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05098)? **A:** The Treachery is not considered active while attached to “Fool me once…”

- **Q:** If I only resolve the surge keyword on a treachery, can I still play “Fool me once...” and attach the treachery to it? **A:** Yes, resolving the surge keyword on a treachery counts as resolving an effect of the card. (Official FAQ v2.5, February 2026)
