# Surprising Find (06278)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-04-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/06278>

**Q:** What happens if you trigger the ***Research*** ability on [Surprising Find](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06278) during a skill test, but before committing cards? (for example, by using [Guided by the Unseen](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07223), [No Stone Unturned](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03307), [Practice Makes Perfect](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06197), non-taboo [Mr. "Rook"](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05153), or [Knowledge is Power](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05231) on [Old Book of Lore](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01031) during the player window before step 2 of the skill test.) Does Surprising Find get committed to the current test, or does it need to wait until the next time a test begins? **A:** ~~The next time. To satisfy the condition “the next eligible skill test” on Surprising Find, you must commit it to an eligible skill test that was not already initiated.~~ If it’s discovered during a skill test, it must be committed to that skill test. (September 2023)
