# Ursula Downs (04002)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-06-05; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/04002>

- **Q:** Does [Ursula Downs'](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04002) [reaction] ability trigger AoOs? **A:** It does. You're doing the full process / rules for taking an action. It's different from something like Yorick's or Leo's ability, if that's the comparison being made. - FAQ, v.1.3, May 2018

- **Q:** Does the 'move' on Ursula's [elder_sign] effect trigger AoOs? **A:** It does not. It's not a **move** action, so behaves like [Shortcut](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02022).

- **Q:** Does the ability on Ursula Downs allow me to take an **investigate** action on an asset or event card? **A:** Yes. Ursula’s reaction allows you to take any investigate action, including those performed via the activate action or via the play action. - FAQ, v.1.3, May 2018
