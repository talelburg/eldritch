# Father Mateo (04004)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-01-02; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/04004>

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata from the Arkham Horror FAQ in its card text, so the ArkhamDB text and the card image above differ, as the ArkhamDB text has been edited to contain this erratum (updated January 2022): **Erratum:** This card’s [reaction] ability should read: “When an investigator reveals an [auto_fail] chaos token..." - FAQ, v.1.4, September 2018

- **Clarification:** If a skill test both automatically succeeds and automatically fails, [for instance by drawing [auto_fail] and [elder_sign] with [Olive McBride](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04197) while playing as [Father Mateo](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04004)], the automatic failure takes precedence, and the test automatically fails. - FAQ, v.1.3, May 2018

- **Automatic Success/Failure:** Some card effects make an investigator automatically succeed or automatically fail a skill test. If this occurs, depending on the timing of such an effect, certain steps of the skill test may be skipped in their entirety.
  - If it is known that an investigator automatically succeeds or fails at a skill test before Step 3 (“[Reveal Chaos Token](../../rules-reference/rules/Appendix_II_Timing_and_Gameplay.md#skill-test-timing)”) occurs, that step is skipped, along with Step 4. No chaos token(s) are revealed from the chaos bag, and the investigator immediately moves to Step 5. All other steps of the skill test resolve as normal.
  - If a chaos token effect causes an investigator to automatically succeed or fail at a skill test, continue with Steps 3 and 4, as normal. - FAQ, v.1.7, March 2020
