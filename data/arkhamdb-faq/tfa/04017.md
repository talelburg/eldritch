# Survival Knife (04017)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-07-03; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/04017>

- **Q:** [Survival Knife](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04017)'s [reaction] ability allows you to perform a **Fight** action against enemies after an enemy deals damage to you during the enemy phase. Can you perform this ability against enemies who are not at your current location such as [Eztli Guardian](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04086) and [Hastur](https://arkhamdb.com/card/03332)? While the Rules Reference does not specifically prohibit this, it also seems to only contemplate performing **Fight** actions against enemies at your location. In other words, is the Survival Knife in fact a cosmic boomerang? **A:** [Survival Knife](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04017)’s [reaction] attack does in fact work against enemies not at your location. Typically an attack can only be initiated against an enemy at your location. This attack is different because it does not allow you the choice of who the target is, instead stating “This attack targets the attacking enemy.” This overrides the typical rules for who an attack can target (similar to how [Stray Cat](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01076) and [Cunning Distraction](https://arkhamdb.com/card/01078) override the typical rules for who an evasion attempt can target).
