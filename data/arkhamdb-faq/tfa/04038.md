# Dark Pact (04038)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-05-25; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/04038>

- **Q:** What happens to [Dark Pact](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04038) when it's removed from your deck? **A:** Dark Pact is returned to your collection, but should not be returned to the pool of available weaknesses when it is swapped out – this ensures that you can always swap between Dark Pact and [The Price of Failure](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04039) without it being unavailable later. Same goes for [Doomed](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04040) and [Accursed Fate](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04041) if they are swapped out. I will make a note to add this to the FAQ.
