# Doomed (04040)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-05-25; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/04040>

- **Q:** What happens to [Doomed](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04040) when it's removed from your deck? **A:** Doomed is returned to your collection, but should not be returned to the pool of available weaknesses when it is swapped out. I will make a note to add this to the FAQ.
