# Fence (04108)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2018-09-14; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/04108>

- **Q:** With [Fence](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04108), can I play an Illicit asset (or appropriate event, such as [Pay Day](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04233)) in the [free] after my last action? This seems a bit more prickly as I need to initiate a play action before I can give fast to the card... But then also fast cards don't use the play Action to enter play?  **A:** Yes, you can. Fast assets can be played in any player window during your turn, including the one after your last action is taken. Fence gives the card fast before it is actually played, so this is taken into account during its initiation sequence.
