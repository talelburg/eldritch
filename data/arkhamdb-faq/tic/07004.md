# Dexter Drake (07004)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-09-01; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/07004>

- **Q:** I have a rules question about [Dexter Drake](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07004). His [elder_sign] effect says "+2. You may return an asset from your play area to your hand. Then, draw 1 card". The 'then' rule indicates that the preceding text must resolve in full in order to perform the effect after 'then'. Is Dexter's ability fully resolved, allowing you to draw a card, even if you choose not to return an asset to your hand? The 'may return' makes it sound like there's a chance the ability is fully resolved after having made the choice not to return an asset. **A:** Dexter Drake would need to return an asset to hand before drawing 1 card with his [elder_sign] ability.
