# Silas Marsh (07005)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-08-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/07005>

- **Q:** If I commit [Daring](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06111) to a skill test as [Silas Marsh](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07005) and use his ability to return it to my hand, do its effects still resolve? **A:** The lasting effect that grants the enemy retaliate and alert would continue for the duration of the skill test, since it is a lasting effect, and lasting effects persist for the specified duration regardless of whether the card that created the lasting effect is or remains in play. The second aspect of the ability, however, functions like any other ability on a skill card; it may only be used during the resolution of the skill test to which Daring is committed. (It is not a delayed effect.) - FAQ, v.2.0, August 2022
