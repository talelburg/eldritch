# Guardian Angel (07006)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-11-09; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/07006>

- **Q:** Can [Sister Mary](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07001) choose to assign damage to [Guardian Angel](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07006), put [bless] tokens in the bag, and then cancel or ignore that damage with a card like ["I've had worse…"](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02261) or [Deny Existence](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05032), not actually placing any damage tokens on Guardian Angel? **A:** Yes, Guardian Angel can be “assigned” the damage, causing [bless] tokens put into the bag, and then the damage can be cancelled or ignored before it’s actually “dealt” to Guardian Angel.
