# Eye of the Djinn (07225)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2025-07-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/07225>

- **Q:** When can I use parallel Father Mateo’s [reaction] ability? **A:** When an investigator with a [bless] token sealed on them would reveal a chaos token for any reason (either via a skill test or when resolving a card such as [Absolution](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10024)), you may trigger Father Mateo’s [reaction] ability for that investigator to use the [bless] token sealed on them for that skill test or card ability (this counts either as “revealing” or “resolving” the token for the purposes of that ability). This interpretation may also be applied to cards such as [Tidal Memento](https://arkhamdb.com/card/90038), [Eye of the Djinn](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07225), and [Book of Living Myths](https://arkhamdb.com/card/10013). (Official FAQ v2.4, July 2025)
