# Ancestral Knowledge (07303)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-01-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303>

- **Q:** Can I look at facedown cards beneath or attached to other cards I control? If I use the [fast] ability on [Ancestral Knowledge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303) to draw one of the cards attached to it, am I allowed to know what each card is, or is it random? **A:** Unless an effect states otherwise, investigators are allowed to look at the other sides of facedown cards beneath or attached to cards they control, such as [Ancestral Knowledge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303), [Backpack](https://arkhamdb.com/card/04037), and [Diana Stanley](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05004). If you are given the ability to draw, play, or interact with one of those cards,
you may look at them to know which one you wish to draw or play. This is not true for facedown cards that are attached to encounter cards or placed facedown via a scenario effect, such as swarm cards, empty space, or tomes beneath locations in the challenge scenario Read or Die. In these instances, players cannot look at the other side of those cards unless instructed otherwise. - FAQ, v.1.9, June 2021

- **Q:** How does [Vincent Lee](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09004) interact with [Ancestral Knowledge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303)? Vincent Lee adds copies of [On the Mend](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09006) to his deck, which are set aside when each game begins - do they count towards the deckbuilding requirement that the deck must contain at least 10 skills? **A:** Vincent Lee’s “On the Mend” skills would not count toward the required 10 skills of Ancestral Knowledge.
