# Shrine of the Moirai (07310)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2022-09-11; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/07310>

- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata from the Arkham Horror FAQ in its card text, so the ArkhamDB text and the card image above differ, as the ArkhamDB text has been edited to contain this erratum (updated August 2022): **Erratum:** The $free ability granted by this card should read: "...Any investigator at this location may trigger this ability." - FAQ, v.2.0, August 2022

- **Q:** Can I return signature cards to my hand with [Shrine of the Moirai](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07310)? If so, how many levels do they count as? **A:** Signature cards do not have a level, so they cannot be returned to your hand with Shrine of the Moirai. - FAQ, v.2.0, August 2022
