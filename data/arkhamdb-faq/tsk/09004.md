# Vincent Lee (09004)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-09-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09004>

- **Q:** How does [Vincent Lee](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09004) interact with [Ancestral Knowledge](https://arkhamdb.com/card/07303)? Vincent Lee adds copies of [On the Mend](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09006) to his deck, which are set aside when each game begins - do they count towards the deckbuilding requirement that the deck must contain at least 10 skills? **A:** Vincent Lee’s “On the Mend” skills would not count toward the required 10 skills of Ancestral Knowledge.

- **Q:** Can [Vincent Lee](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09004) upgrade [Hallowed Mirror](https://arkhamdb.com/card/54002) (3)? **A:** ~~Yes, Vincent can upgrade Hallowed Mirror.~~ Bonded cards **cannot** be treated as part of the text box of the card to which they are bonded. (August 2023)
