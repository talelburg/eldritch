# Burden of Leadership (09020)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-02-28; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09020>

-- **NB:** ArkhamDB now incorporates errata from the Arkham Horror FAQ in its card text, so the ArkhamDB text and the card image above differ, as the ArkhamDB text has been edited to contain this erratum (updated February 2024): **Erratum:** This card’s **Revelation** ability should read: “…Otherwise, for each Ally asset you control, you must either exhaust it or deal it 1 direct damage and 1 direct horror.”" - FAQ, v.2.2, February 2024 [What follows below is two ruling requests exploring this topic.]

- **Q:** I heard today that with [Burden of Leadership](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09020), since there's no ''must'' in the text, you can choose to exhaust an ally that's already exhausted to prevent them from taking the damage/horror. ~~I reach out to you because I feel it kind of strange to be allowed to do this and would like to know if it's the intent of the card or the designers forgot to include the ''must'' to force a choice that can alter the game state? **A:** You cannot exhaust a card that’s already exhausted—this is stated outright in the rules reference under [Exhaust, Exhausted](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Exhaust.md). If you have Ally assets that are already exhausted when you resolve Burden of Leadership, you must deal the damage and horror to them.~~ **Ruling updated**: Though there exists a rule under [Exhausted](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Exhaust.md) stating that an exhausted card cannot be exhausted again until it is ready, this rule doesn’t have much bearing when there is also a rule for [Must](../../rules-reference/rules/glossary/Must.md) stating that its absence when choosing between multiple options allows the player to choose “an option that does not change the game state.” Thus, the rules of Must conflict with the intention of Burden of Leadership. For that reason, [Burden of Leadership](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09020) will be receiving an errata, where “must” will be included in the presented choice: *“For each Ally asset you control, you must either exhaust it or deal it 1 direct damage and 1 direct horror.”*

- **Q:** In late 2022, there was a ruling on Charlie's weakness, [Burden of Leadership](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09020).  In July of this year, many months after that ruling, the new v2.1 FAQ was released and the new FAQ did not include this errata. Does this mean that the Burden of Leadership ruling was reversed and there is no inclusion of "must"? Or should this errata have been included in the new FAQ and it was an oversight not to include it? **A:** No, the ruling wasn’t reversed—it was an oversight on our part not to include it in the latest FAQ. Burden of Leadership needs an erratum where its ability states “…for each Ally asset you control, you must either exhaust it or deal it 1 direct damage and 1 direct horror.” Please apply this ruling to the card if you play Charlie. (October, 2023)
