# Grievous Wound (09027)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-09-29; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09027>

- **Q:** After initiating a standard fight action or triggered fight ability on a non-melee asset can the reaction ability on [Ice Pick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105) be used to play [Grievous Wound](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09027)? **A:** No. With [Grievous Wound](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09027), you need to have initiated a fight/attack with an ability on a melee asset. [Ice Pick](https://arkhamdb.com/card/08105) does not initiate a fight/attack. (August 2023)
