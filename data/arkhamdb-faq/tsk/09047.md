# Captivating Discovery (09047)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-03-17; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09047>

- **Q:** How/can [Mandy Thompson](https://arkhamdb.com/card/06002) resolve her reaction ability to "resolve an additional target" when playing [Captivating Discovery](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09047)? Does Captivating Discovery inherently have any search targets? **A:** If Mandy Thompson plays Captivating Discovery, she’d either search the top 9 cards of her deck, or, for one clue she placed she could add 3 of the searched cards to her hand instead of 2. (For just one clue, not all three.)
