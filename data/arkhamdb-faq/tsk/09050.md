# Lab Coat (09050)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-12-16; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09050>

- **Q:** I would like to know how the automatic failure and the Lab Coat interact. We have some doubts about what happens if the test's difficulty is 1 or 0. Example: - I have Lab Coat and [Alchemical Distillation](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09040) in play. - I activate the Alchemical Distillation and make an Intellect test with difficulty 1. - I draw the automatic failure. What would happen in this case, can I activate the Lab Coat and succeed by 0? Or since I drew the automatic failure, would I fail anyway? **A:** If you failed the Alchemical Distillation test by pulling the Autofail token, you could exhaust Lab Coat to instead succeed by 0. (Rules Form, December 2023)
