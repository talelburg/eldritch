# Orphic Theory (09051)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-01-13; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09051>

- **Q:** How does [Orphic Theory](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09051) interact with Hidden cards? Hidden cards are treachery cards treated as if they are in your threat area - can I target a hidden card in my hand with Orphic Theory? Does that remove the Hidden keyword and allow the card to be discarded via some other means? **A:** Orphic Theory cannot target Hidden treacheries in your hand.
