# Stylish Coat (09071)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2024-02-25; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09071>

- **Q:** Does [Stylish Coat](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09071) trigger with [Lone Wolf](https://arkhamdb.com/card/02188)? Specifically, is 'when your turn begins' still during your turn? **A:** Yes, Stylish Coat can be triggered by Lone Wolf. “When your turn begins” still counts as occurring “during your turn.”

- **Q:** What types of abilities can cause me to trigger the [reaction] ability on [Stylish Coat](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09071)? **A:** The [reaction] ability on Stylish Coat can be used with a card effect specifically instructs you to “gain” resources, such as with [Salvage](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09116). Any other card abilities that “place” resources, such as with [.45 Thompson (3)](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05186), or "move" resources from one card to another or from one card to your resource pool, such as the ability on [Family Inheritance](https://arkhamdb.com/card/05011), do not trigger Stylish Coat's ability. (FAQ v2.2, February 2024)
