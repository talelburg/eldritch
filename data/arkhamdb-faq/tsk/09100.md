# Makeshift Trap (09100)

Rulings last updated on ArkhamDB 2023-08-25; fetched 2026-08-15. Source: <https://arkhamdb.com/card/09100>

**Q:** If Parallel Ashcan Pete owns a copy of [Makeshift Trap](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09100) with **Explosive Device**, and he chooses to trigger his reaction ability when the card would be discarded, does the **Explosive Device** damage still happen? **A:** Parallel Ashcan Pete’s reaction ability triggers when an attached card “would be discarded,” which has a higher timing priority than “is discarded,” and replaces the discarding of that card. As such, Parallel Pete adding [Makeshift Trap](https://arkhamdb.com/card/09100) to his hand would make it so the **Explosive Device** upgrade does not trigger. (Rules Q&A, August 2023)
