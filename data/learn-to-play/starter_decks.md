# Investigator Starter Decks

Verbatim from p. 4 of the 2016 Core Set *Learn to Play*
([`ahc01_learn_to_play_web.pdf`](ahc01_learn_to_play_web.pdf); provenance in
[`SOURCE.md`](SOURCE.md)). The sidebar prints the two decks as two columns; they
are transcribed here one after the other, in the printed order. Typographic
quotes and apostrophes are as printed.

The note beside the sidebar, at the top of the page's right-hand column:

> Some cards have an upgraded version with one or more white pips in the upper
> left-hand corner of the card. When building these starter decks, **do not**
> include these upgraded versions.

and its caption on the pictured card:

> When building starter decks, only use cards that have no white pips in this
> area.
