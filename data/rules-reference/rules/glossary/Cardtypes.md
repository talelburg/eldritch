# Cardtypes

The game's cardtypes are presented in Appendix IV, with detailed card anatomies (see "[Appendix IV: Card Anatomy](../Appendix_IV_Card_Anatomy.md)" on page 28).

- If an ability causes a card to change its cardtype, it loses all other cardtypes it might possess and functions as would any card of the new cardtype.
