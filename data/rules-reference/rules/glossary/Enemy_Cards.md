# Enemy Cards

Enemies represent villains, cultists, ne'er-do-wells, terrible monsters, and unfathomable entities from alternate dimensions or the cosmos beyond. When an enemy card is drawn by an investigator, that investigator must spawn it following any spawn direction the card bears (see "[Spawn](Spawn.md)" on page 19). If the encountered enemy has no spawn direction, the enemy spawns engaged with the investigator encountering the card and is placed in that investigator's threat area.

See "[1.4 Each investigator draws 1 encounter card](../Appendix_II_Timing_and_Gameplay.md#i-mythos-phase)" on page 24.

- A ready, unengaged enemy engages any time it is at the same location as an investigator (see "[Enemy Engagement](Enemy_Engagement.md)" on page 10).
- If an investigator is engaged with a ready enemy and takes an action other than to **fight**, to **evade**, or to activate a **parley** or **resign** ability, that enemy makes an attack of opportunity (see "[Attack of Opportunity](Attack_of_Opportunity.md)" on page 5).
- Enemies with the hunter keyword move during the Enemy Phase (see "[III. Enemy phase](../Appendix_II_Timing_and_Gameplay.md#iii-enemy-phase)" on page 25).
- Engaged enemies attack during the Enemy Phase (see "[III. Enemy phase](../Appendix_II_Timing_and_Gameplay.md#iii-enemy-phase)" on page 25).
